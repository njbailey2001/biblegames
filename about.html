About

Bible Games Daily is a small collection of free games for people who know their Bible, or want to know it better. Every game runs right in your browser. There's nothing to download and no account to make.

The games

  • Daily Verse Quest: read a verse and find its book, chapter and verse in five guesses.
  • Cross Reference: find the hidden word from Scripture. Each guess is ranked by how closely it connects to the answer.
  • Bible Crossword: a new 9×9 crossword built from Scripture every day.
  • Build-a-Figure: spin biblical figures and combine their strengths into one. Play solo or pass the phone around.

Why it's free

The site is supported by ads. We block ad categories that don't belong on a family-friendly Bible site. If you ever see an ad that seems out of place, please let us know.

Scripture

Verse text in Daily Verse Quest comes from the American Standard Version (ASV), which is in the public domain. Crossword answers follow the NIV.
