How to play

Name the book, chapter, and verse of the passage in five guesses. When you finish, hit Next verse and keep going as long as you like.

  • Book turns green when right, gold when it's in the same section (Gospels, Pauline Epistles, Minor Prophets…). The arrow says whether the answer comes earlier ↑ or later ↓ in the Bible.
  • Chapter turns gold within 3, verse within 5. Arrows point higher ↑ or lower ↓.
  • Hint reveals the testament, then the section. Hints are free but noted in your result.
  • Daily Verse is one verse per day, the same for everyone, and builds a day-by-day streak. Come back tomorrow for the next one.
  • In Unlimited, Easy draws from well-known verses; Hard draws from less familiar ones. Daily, Easy and Hard each keep their own stats and streak.
